Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: Clearwell does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything happens on your iPhone or iPad.

Your screenshots

You choose a screenshot of your Home Screen with the system photo picker, which gives Clearwell only that one image, not your library. Clearwell keeps a copy in its own storage on your device so you can come back to it, and cuts the pieces of wallpaper from it there. It is never uploaded.

Icons and widgets

Icons you save go to the Clearwell folder in the Files app and, if you allow it, to your photo library. Clearwell asks only to add photos, not to read them. Images you put on an icon are chosen with the system photo picker in the same way. Widget pieces are kept in storage shared only between the Clearwell app and its own widget. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. Deleting a Home Screen in Clearwell deletes its screenshot and widget pieces; deleting the app removes everything except the icons you saved to Files or Photos.

Purchases

Clearwell Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Clearwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it only asks Apple whether you bought it. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Clearwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data. Clearwell works with the network turned off.

Children

Clearwell is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.
