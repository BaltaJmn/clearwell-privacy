Clearwell

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

In short: Clearwell does not collect your content, and has no account, ads or tracking. Everything you make stays on your iPhone or iPad. The optional weather widget (Pro) sends your approximate location, or a city you type, to Apple Weather to get the weather (see Weather widget). Purchases and a few anonymous usage counters go through RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

Your screenshots

You choose a screenshot of your Home Screen with the system photo picker, which gives Clearwell only that one image, not your library. Clearwell keeps a copy in its own storage on your device so you can come back to it, and cuts the pieces of wallpaper from it there. It is never uploaded.

Icons and widgets

Icons you save go to the Clearwell folder in the Files app and, if you allow it, to your photo library. Clearwell asks only to add photos, not to read them. Images you put on an icon are chosen with the system photo picker in the same way. Widget pieces are kept in storage shared only between the Clearwell app and its own widget. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. Deleting a Home Screen in Clearwell deletes its screenshot and widget pieces; deleting the app removes everything except the icons you saved to Files or Photos.

Weather widget (Pro)

The weather widget is optional and part of Clearwell Pro. When you choose it, Clearwell asks iOS for permission to use your location, and only then. Only your approximate location is used, and only to get the weather: it is sent to Apple Weather (Apple's WeatherKit service), which answers with the temperature and conditions. Clearwell does not store it and does not send it to us or to anyone else.

If you prefer, type a city instead of allowing location. Clearwell then looks the city up with Apple's location services, so the name you typed goes to Apple, and the place it finds goes to Apple Weather. If you refuse the permission, or take it back in iOS Settings, the weather widget needs a typed city. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. Legal attribution and Apple Weather's policy: weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html.

Purchases

Clearwell Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. Clearwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether you bought it. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Clearwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Apart from the anonymous counters described next, nothing else from Clearwell, such as your screenshots, icons or widgets, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Purchases and usage. Clearwell uses RevenueCat to keep track of purchases. With it, the app sends a few anonymous counters: how many times the Pro screen was shown and closed without buying, which screen it was opened from, how many times a soft Pro suggestion was shown, and in which version of the app the rating prompt was shown. These numbers are used only to understand and improve how the app is offered. They carry no content from the app, no name, email or other identifier, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. Nothing is sent in test builds.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Clearwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

What Clearwell does not do

No account, no advertising, no tracking. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, which handles purchases and the few anonymous counters described under Purchases. Clearwell works with the network turned off, except for the weather widget, which needs it, and the purchase record and the anonymous counters, which are sent to RevenueCat when the device is online. If you install icons as Web Clips, Clearwell hands the file to Safari through a small server that runs only inside your iPhone, at the address 127.0.0.1. It answers once and then stops, and nothing goes out to the internet.

Children

Clearwell is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID, the purchase record and the anonymous counters (see Purchases).
