Clearwell

Icons and widgets you can see through

Clearwell cuts your wallpaper into the exact pieces that sit under each icon and widget spot of your Home Screen. Put them there and the spot disappears into the wallpaper: empty gaps where you want them, floating symbols, widgets with nothing but the time on your wallpaper.

Lined up on your iPhone, not on a guess. Every icon and widget spot was measured on each iPhone and iPad screen, on iOS 26 and iOS 27, with small and with large icons. The pieces come straight from your screenshot's pixels, never resized, so they stay sharp.

How it works

Take a screenshot of an empty Home Screen page (touch and hold the Home Screen, swipe past your last page) and choose it in Clearwell. The spots appear on it. Tap one:

Icons: leave it blank, put a symbol in any color on it, or your own image (a PNG with a transparent background shows the wallpaper around it). Save it, and Clearwell walks you through Apple's Shortcuts app to put it on your Home Screen, step by step.

Widgets: small, medium, large and, on iPad, extra large. Choose nothing, the time, the date or your own text on top, then add the Clearwell widget. It already shows your newest piece of that size: no Shortcuts at all.

iPhone and iPad

Every iPhone that runs iOS 26 or 27, from the iPhone SE and 11 to the 17 and 18 Pro Max and iPhone Air, and every iPad, in portrait and in landscape.

Your first Home Screen is free, with everything. Clearwell Pro, for as many Home Screens as you like (each page, Dark Mode, a Focus with its own wallpaper), is a one-time purchase: $2.99 in the US (prices vary by country). No subscription.
